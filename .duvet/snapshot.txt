SPECIFICATION: [Kotlin Line Classifier: Specification](design/classifiers/kotlin-spec.md)
  SECTION: [1. Grammar](#grammar)
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: The classifier MUST parse Kotlin source with the `tree-sitter-kotlin-ng`
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: grammar.

  SECTION: [2. Pre-pass and post-pass](#pre-and-post-pass)
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: Before the CST walk, the classifier MUST mark
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: blank lines as `Whitespace`
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: and duvet annotation lines (`//=` or `//#` after leading whitespace)
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: as `Annotation`.
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: After the CST walk, the classifier MUST apply the verified
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: mutual-exclusivity post-pass (`clean_classifications`)
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: to every classification it returns.

  SECTION: [3. Parse errors](#parse-errors)
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: When the parse tree contains any `ERROR` or `MISSING` node,
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: the classifier MUST return `Unclassifiable`
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: and MUST report the line of every `ERROR` and `MISSING` node.

  SECTION: [4. Scope-bearing nodes](#scope-bearing-nodes)
    TEXT[implementation,test]: The scope-bearing node kinds are
    TEXT[implementation,test]: `block`, `class_body`, `enum_class_body`, `lambda_literal`,
    TEXT[implementation,test]: and `when_expression`.
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: For each scope-bearing node, the classifier MUST mark
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: `ScopeOpen` on the line of its opening brace token
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: and `ScopeClose` on the line of its closing brace token.
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: The scope-event stream MUST contain one open and one close event
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: per scope-bearing node, keyed at the byte offsets of its brace tokens,
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: in source order.
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: `when_expression` and `lambda_literal` carry their brace tokens
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: as direct children rather than in a child `block`;
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: their events MUST be keyed on those tokens.

  SECTION: [5.1 Declarations with scope](#declarations-with-scope)
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: The node kinds
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: `class_declaration`, `object_declaration`, `companion_object`,
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: `function_declaration`, `secondary_constructor`,
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: `anonymous_initializer`, `getter`, and `setter`
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: MUST be marked with the declaration-with-scope treatment:
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: every line from the node's start to the line before its body
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: is marked `Declaration`,
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: and the body child supplies the scope events.
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: A function header line MUST NOT be marked `Statement`,
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: including when its parameters carry default-value expressions.
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: Class-like headers (including `data`, `sealed`, `enum`, and
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: `annotation` classes, interfaces, objects, and companion objects)
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: carry JaCoCo's synthesized-member and primary-constructor probes;
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: they MUST be marked `Declaration` and MUST NOT be marked `Statement`.

  SECTION: [5.2 Declarations without scope](#declarations-without-scope)
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: The node kinds
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: `package_header`, `import`, `type_alias`, and `annotation`
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: MUST mark their span `Declaration`.
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: A `function_declaration` with neither a brace-form body
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: nor an expression body (an abstract or interface member)
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: MUST mark its span `Declaration` only.

  SECTION: [5.3 Properties](#properties)
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: A `property_declaration` MUST mark its span `Declaration`.
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: When it carries an initializer or a `property_delegate` child,
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: it MUST additionally mark `Statement`
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: from its start line through the end line
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: of the initializer or delegate,
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: and MUST NOT mark `Statement` on any accessor line after it.

  SECTION: [5.4 Enum entries](#enum-entries)
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: An `enum_entry` MUST mark its span
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: with both `Declaration` and `Statement`,
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: with or without constructor arguments.

  SECTION: [6.1 Expression-body functions](#expression-body-functions)
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: A `function_body` in `=` form (expression body)
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: MUST mark the expression's span `Statement`.

  SECTION: [6.2 Statement-position expressions](#statement-position-expressions)
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: The node kinds
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: `call_expression`, `navigation_expression`, `assignment`,
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: `return_expression`, and `throw_expression`
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: MUST mark their span `Statement`.

  SECTION: [6.3 Control headers](#control-headers)
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: The node kinds
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: `if_expression`, `when_expression`,
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: `for_statement`, and `while_statement`
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: MUST mark `Statement` on their start line only.
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: A `do_while_statement` MUST mark `Statement` on its end line
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: (the `} while (condition)` line) and MUST NOT mark its start line.

  SECTION: [6.4 When arms](#when-arms)
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: A `when_entry` MUST mark `Statement` on its start line.

  SECTION: [6.5 Try/catch/finally headers](#try-catch-finally)
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: `try_expression`, `catch_block`, and `finally_block`
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: MUST mark `Declaration` on their start lines;
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: their `block` children supply the scopes.

  SECTION: [7. Non-linear control](#non-linear-control)
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: A `label` node (a label declaration such as `outer@` or `lit@`)
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: MUST mark `NonLinearControl` on its line.
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: A `labeled_expression` (`break@label`, `continue@label`)
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: MUST mark `NonLinearControl` on its line.
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: A `return_expression` whose first token is `return@`
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: (a labeled return) MUST be marked `Statement`,
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: not `NonLinearControl`.

  SECTION: [8. Comments](#comments)
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: `line_comment` and `block_comment` nodes
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: MUST mark their span `Comment`,
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: except lines already marked `Annotation` by the pre-pass.

  SECTION: [9. Unmodeled constructs](#unmodeled-constructs)
    TEXT[implementation,test]: Any node kind this document does not name
    TEXT[implementation,test]: contributes no property of its own;
    TEXT[implementation,test]: its lines keep whatever enclosing nodes marked,
    TEXT[implementation,test]: or remain `None` (unknown) per the generic contract's Decision 9.
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: Every arm of the classifier that marks a code or structural property
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: MUST record the marked node's start line as a code-start line
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: for the post-pass's `{code, Comment}` disambiguation.

SPECIFICATION: [Duvet Coverage Model: Formal Specification](design/query/coverage-model-spec.md)
  SECTION: [1.3 Classification Function and Unknown Lines](#classification-function)
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: A line classified as `{Annotation}` MUST NOT also have
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: `Statement` or `Declaration` in its property set.
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: Classifiers MUST apply a post-processing pass
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: after AST classification:
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: for any line that has `Annotation`, `Comment`, or `Whitespace`,
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: remove `Statement` and `Declaration` from its property set.

  SECTION: [1.5 Scopes](#scopes)
    TEXT[test]: A scope is a contiguous range of lines
    TEXT[test]: delimited by `ScopeOpen` and `ScopeClose` properties.
    TEXT[test]: Scopes nest.
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: **Scope Balance Contract:** {#scope-balance-contract}
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: A classifier MUST emit a balanced `ScopeOpen`/`ScopeClose` stream:
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: matching each `ScopeClose` against the most recent unmatched `ScopeOpen`,
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: no `ScopeClose` occurs with no open to match,
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: and no `ScopeOpen` is left unmatched at end of file.
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: When the stream is unbalanced,
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: the coverage model MUST NOT score annotations against the collapsed scope tree;
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: it MUST surface the file as a defeated classification and escalate
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: (see [Classifier Selection and Dispatch](#dispatch)).

  SECTION: [5. Correctness Properties](#correctness-properties)
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: These properties MUST be proven with Verus.
    TEXT[!MUST,implication]: The Verus proof files MUST carry duvet annotations
    TEXT[!MUST,implication]: linking each `proof fn` back to the corresponding property section
    TEXT[!MUST,implication]: in this document.

  SECTION: [Property 1: No False Positives](#property-1-no-false-positives)
    TEXT[!MUST,implication]: The implementation MUST prove that if
    TEXT[!MUST,implication]: `is_annotation_executed(annotation, ...) = Executed`,
    TEXT[!MUST,implication]: then there exists a line L such that:
    TEXT[implication]: - `coverage[L] == Hit`
    TEXT[implication]:   (L is directly reported as executed)
    TEXT[implication]: - L is in the same scope as the annotation's target
    TEXT[implication]: - Every line between L and the annotation's target (exclusive)
    TEXT[implication]:   is classified (`Some`)
    TEXT[implication]:   and has properties that are a subset of
    TEXT[implication]:   {Whitespace, Comment, Annotation, Declaration}
    TEXT[implication]: - No line between L and the annotation's target
    TEXT[implication]:   has the `ScopeClose` property
    TEXT[implication]: - No line between L and the annotation's target
    TEXT[implication]:   has the `ScopeOpen` property
    TEXT[implication]: - No line between L and the annotation's target
    TEXT[implication]:   is unknown (`None`)

  SECTION: [Property 2: No Cross-Scope Leakage](#property-2-no-cross-scope-leakage)
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,implication,test]: The implementation MUST prove that
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,implication,test]: for any two lines A and B
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,implication,test]: where A is in scope S1 and B is in scope S2
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,implication,test]: and S1 ≠ S2
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,implication,test]: and S1 is not a parent of S2
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,implication,test]: and S2 is not a parent of S1:

  SECTION: [Property 3: Conservative Fallback](#property-3-conservative-fallback)
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,implication,test]: The implementation MUST prove that
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,implication,test]: no backward propagation occurs WITHIN a scope
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,implication,test]: that contains a `NonLinearControl` line.
    TEXT[!MAY,implication,test]: If an ancestor scope S contains `NonLinearControl`
    TEXT[!MAY,implication,test]: but a child scope S' does not,
    TEXT[!MAY,implication,test]: propagation MAY occur through S'.

  SECTION: [Property 4: Monotonicity](#property-4-monotonicity)
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,implication,test]: The implementation MUST prove that
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,implication,test]: given two coverage reports E1 and E2
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,implication,test]: where E1 ⊆ E2
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,implication,test]: (E2 reports all the same hits as E1, plus possibly more):

  SECTION: [Property 5: Annotation Stacking Transitivity](#property-5-stacking-transitivity)
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: The implementation MUST prove that
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,implication,test]: if annotation A (lines a1..a2) is immediately above
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,implication,test]: annotation B (lines b1..b2)
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,implication,test]: with only whitespace, comments, or other annotations between them
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: ,
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: and `is_annotation_executed(B, ...) = Executed`,
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: then `is_annotation_executed(A, ...) = Executed`.

  SECTION: [Property 6: Unknown Safety](#property-6-unknown-safety)
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: The implementation MUST prove that
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: unknown lines cannot produce false positives.

  SECTION: [Property 7: Target Determinism](#property-7-target-determinism)
    TEXT[implication]: `annotation_target` is a pure function:
    TEXT[implication]: given the same annotation, classifications, and file length,
    TEXT[implication]: it always returns the same result.
    TEXT[implication]: This is free in Verus
    TEXT[implication]: (all `fn` in Verus are deterministic by construction).

  SECTION: [Property 8: Scope Well-formedness](#property-8-scope-well-formedness)
    TEXT[implication]: `build_scope_tree` produces a well-formed scope tree:
    TEXT[implication]: - Every scope has `open_line <= close_line`.
    TEXT[implication]: - If two scopes overlap,
    TEXT[implication]:   one strictly contains the other (proper nesting).
    TEXT[implication]:   No partial overlaps.

  SECTION: [Property 9: Execution Set Containment](#property-9-execution-set-containment)
    TEXT[test]: The execution set always contains all directly-hit lines.
    TEXT[implication]: If `coverage[line] == Hit`,
    TEXT[implication]: then `line ∈ execution_set(classifications, scopes, coverage)`.

  SECTION: [Property 10: Annotation Target Bounds](#property-10-annotation-target-bounds)
    TEXT[implication]: If `annotation_target(annotation, ...) = Some(target)`,
    TEXT[implication]: then `target.line_number > annotation.end_line`.

  SECTION: [Property 11: Scope-Stream Balance Detection](#property-11-scope-stream-balance-detection)
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: The implementation MUST prove that the balance detector returns balanced if and
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: only if the `ScopeOpen`/`ScopeClose` stream over the classified lines is balanced:
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: no `ScopeClose` occurs while the scope depth is zero, and the depth is zero at
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: end of file.
    TEXT[test]: A stream with no scope delimiters is balanced, and its whole-file scope is
    TEXT[test]: legitimate.

  SECTION: [7.4 Properties](#degraded-properties)
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: These properties MUST be proven with Verus for the degraded path,
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: alongside the Section 5 properties for the classified path.

  SECTION: [Property D1: Direct Observation](#property-d1-direct-observation)
    TEXT[!MUST,implication]: The implementation MUST prove that the degraded status is a direct observation
    TEXT[!MUST,implication]: of the target line's own coverage, never an inference propagated from another
    TEXT[!MUST,implication]: line.

  SECTION: [Property D2: Degraded Target Bounds](#property-d2-degraded-target-bounds)
    TEXT[!MUST,implication]: The implementation MUST prove that any `Executed` or `NotExecuted` degraded
    TEXT[!MUST,implication]: status resolves a target strictly below the annotation
    TEXT[!MUST,implication]: (`target > annotation.end_line`).

  SECTION: [Property D3: Degraded Stacking Transitivity](#property-d3-degraded-stacking)
    TEXT[!MUST,implication]: The implementation MUST prove that the degraded status depends on the annotation
    TEXT[!MUST,implication]: only through its resolved target: two annotations that resolve to the same
    TEXT[!MUST,implication]: target receive the same status.

  SECTION: [Property D4: Agreement with the Classified Model](#property-d4-agreement-with-classified)
    TEXT[!MUST,implication]: The implementation MUST prove that where the degraded path emits a verdict on a
    TEXT[!MUST,implication]: directly-hit line that a full classifier would mark as a plain `Statement` (and
    TEXT[!MUST,implication]: not `NonLinearControl`), it returns the same result the classified model
    TEXT[!MUST,implication]: `is_annotation_executed` returns, namely `Executed`.

  SECTION: [8.2 Trust Taxonomy and Response](#trust-taxonomy)
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: duvet MUST NOT silently substitute the coarse model or score against
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]:   the collapsed scope tree;
    TEXT[!MUST,implementation,test]: it MUST escalate, reporting each located issue.

  SECTION: [8.3 Escalation](#escalation)
    TEXT[!MUST,exception]: When `report` consumes coverage, a defeated commitment MUST be a hard
    TEXT[!MUST,exception]: error there, because `report` is the authoritative artifact.
